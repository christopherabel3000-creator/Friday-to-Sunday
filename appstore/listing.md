## Description (4,000)

Friday to Sunday is a football coaching career you play on your own. You start as the head coach of a tiny small-town high school. Win, and bigger schools call. Keep winning, and colleges call, then the pros.

CALL EVERY PLAY
Pick your personnel, read the defense, and call the play on every snap, on offense and defense. Disguise your coverage, audible at the line, go for it on fourth and one. Or let the computer coach the game until it's close late, then take over for the key moments.

RUN THE PROGRAM
Plan practice every week. Install your schemes, set your depth chart, and make a game plan for each opponent. Every offense and defense has plays of its own, so the scheme you pick is a real choice.

CLIMB THE LADDER
Six high school classes, about 670 college programs from Division III to the power conferences, and a 32-team pro league. Earn a reputation, take offers, negotiate contracts, or turn a job down and build something where you are.

COLLEGE AND THE PROS
Recruit with a board, visits and NIL money. Work the transfer portal. In the pros, manage the salary cap, the draft, free agency and trades, with a general manager until you earn control of the roster.

REAL RULES AT EVERY LEVEL
High school, college and pro games each follow their own rules, from kickoffs and overtime to the clock.

MADE FOR VOICEOVER
Friday to Sunday was designed from the start to be fully playable with VoiceOver. Every screen, every play call and every result is read clearly, so you can coach a whole career without looking at the screen.

YOUR GAME, YOUR WAY
Every school, conference and team is made up, and you can rename any of them. The game works offline and saves as you play. Optional cloud saves and save codes let you move your career to another device.

No ads. No accounts. No tracking.

- Data type: User Content, then Gameplay Content.

## Notes for the reviewer

Friday to Sunday is a complete single-player football coaching game, built to be fully playable with VoiceOver by a developer who plays with a screen reader. The whole game is bundled in the app and runs offline. Saves are kept on the device (including as files in the app's Documents folder). The only network use is optional cloud saves (Cloud saves and save codes, on the main menu), which use Google Firebase.

To try it: tap Start a career, enter any coach name, tap Find a job, then pick any school. Sim the rest of camp, then tap Play the game to call plays, or Key moments to take over late in a close game. No account or sign-in is needed.
